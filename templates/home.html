Trekking System

Manage treks, staff, and bookings in one place.

Main Features

Features planned for the trekking system

Browse Treks

Users can view open treks with details like location, difficulty, duration, and slots.

Easy Booking

Users can book available treks and view their booking status later.

Staff Management

Admin can manage trek staff and assign staff members to treks.

Trek History

The system will maintain booking and trekking history for users and the admin.

How the Application Works

This application will connect three main roles: Admin, Trek Staff, and User. Admin manages the system, staff manages assigned treks, and users book treks.

Admin: Manages treks, users, staff, and bookings.

Staff: Updates assigned trek status and participants.

User: Browses and books available treks.
